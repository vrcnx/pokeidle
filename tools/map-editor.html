How to use

Cities: pick the City tool, then click on the map to drop a marker — choose which city it represents from the dropdown.

Routes: pick the Route tool, click each waypoint along the route, double-click or press Enter to finish, then label it.

Edit: pick Select tool, then drag any marker. Right-click a marker or use the list on the right to delete.
